Page 1 — origin story

An ordinary
codebase…

…until you multiply it by thirty-four. The lesson took four companies to learn: at one app a hardcoded value is a shortcut you regret mildly; at thirty-four it is a store review, a staged rollout and a week of waiting. Scale does not break your architecture — it stops letting you get away with not having one.
